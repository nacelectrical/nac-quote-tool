Customers & jobs

Turns the customer names already on NAC's quotes and designs into real CUSTOMER and JOB records, and links them up: customer → job → design → quote.

Before you press anything

This reads first and shows you exactly what it would do. Nothing is written until you press APPLY. When it does write, it only ever fills in blanks: a value NAC already has is kept, and any disagreement is listed below for you to settle yourself. Existing quotes, designs and the customer signing page are not changed in any way.

If the nac_customers and nac_jobs tables do not exist yet, run designer/crm-schema.sql in the Supabase SQL editor first. It is additive — it drops nothing and renames nothing.
